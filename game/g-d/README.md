# Lane G-D: Godot 4.7.2 3D hybrid (THROWAWAY)

THROWAWAY: this project exists only to run the Phase 1 bake-off ([docs/bakeoff/README.md](../../docs/bakeoff/README.md)). Mechanics are undecided (decision 0009); nothing here is canon.

- `bash game/g-d/ci.sh` runs every check and writes `bakeoff/g-d/reports/`.
- Play: `godot --path game/g-d` (keys in [mechanic.md](../../docs/bakeoff/mechanic.md#controls)).
- Replay with a trace: `godot --headless --fixed-fps 60 --path game/g-d res://tests/replay_runner.tscn -- --replay=res://replays/level01.replay.json --trace`.
- `bakeoff/{actions,resonance,greybox,replay,resonance_tag,tag_validator}.gd` and `tests/*.gd` are lane G-A's, copied with `jj file show -r bakeoff/g-a game/g-a/<path>` (the tests with `Game2D`/`Node2D` renamed to `Game3D`/`Node3D`). A G-A fix is re-copied the same way.
- The world is 1 m per tile on the z = 0 plane (docs/bakeoff/greybox-format.md); `GreyboxArt3D` draws toon-shaded cubes (the default art the replay and tag checks build with), and the STAND-IN capsule until the protagonist rig is in the project.
- Look: `art/lane_art.tres` is `KitArt3D` (`art/kit_art_3d.gd`), which dresses the level with the desert kit in `kit/` (copied from PR #31, see `kit/README.md`): sand tiles where a solid cell is open above and rock tiles beneath, solid 1 m tag-blocks tinted per mechanic.md on tagged cells (gray until the color is acquired; `art/tag_block.gdshader` adds stone grain, a gradient and a carved glyph, a diamond for red and a ring for green; lit only by a neutral white key, so they render close to the brief's colours; the warm sun, the fills and the blocks' own lights skip them), spikes, an orb pedestal that stays when the orb is taken, the goal gate, and seeded (20260927) mesas, arches, dunes, boulders, ruins, saguaros and acacias behind the play plane, hazed toward the horizon per layer, with a field of rolling dunes in the middle distance and a pale horizon layer of differently stretched mesas and arches further back, sand drifts at the foot of each step, small rocks, saguaros and scrub along the platform tops, a broad warm-to-cool colour drift across the ground, hazed acacia and saguaro silhouettes in the foreground, flapping birds, tiles varied in world-space bands of tone and edged with a dark lip where they are walkable, and the painted sky `art/sky.png` (`gen image`, glow-up round 1) on a quad that follows the camera, where the fogged sand meets it. Violet is drawn 0.6 m in front of the play plane with an even dark outline, lit by her own warm key light and not by the tag-blocks' coloured lights, so walls never hide or tint her. It changes no collider. `assets/bakeoff/desert-kit-3d/build_kit.py` and its sidecar are there only because the GLB sidecars name it as their input and `provenance check` follows inputs.
- Character: `bakeoff/rig.gd` and `bakeoff/rig_animator.gd` are lane G-A's, copied unchanged the same way; `bakeoff/rig.gd` reads the protagonist rig (the Spine 4.3 JSON subset in [character-rig.md](../../docs/bakeoff/character-rig.md), no Spine runtime) and `art/rig_character_3d.gd` draws it, one shaded Sprite3D per part in the player's XY plane, 1.6 m tall. `attach_character` uses it once `assets/bakeoff/protagonist/` (`rig/violet.json`, `rig/violet.meta.json`, `parts/*.png` with their sidecars) is copied to `protagonist/`; a rig that is there but does not load stops the game with exit 1 and the error, never falling back to the STAND-IN. Facing left mirrors each sprite rather than the root (character-rig.md's negative root x scale turns the shaded sprites black).
- Rig reader tests: `godot --headless --path game/g-d res://tests/rig_test.tscn` (in `ci.sh`; `tests/rig_test.gd` and `tests/rig/` are G-A's). `bash game/g-d/tests/rig/make-fixture.sh` regenerates their fixture (set `SPINERIG=` to a `tools/spinerig` that has `render.py`).
- `ci-tools` lists the mise tools the bakeoff workflow installs for this lane.
