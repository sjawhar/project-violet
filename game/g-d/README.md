# Lane G-D: Godot 4.7.2 3D hybrid (THROWAWAY)

THROWAWAY: this project exists only to run the Phase 1 bake-off ([docs/bakeoff/README.md](../../docs/bakeoff/README.md)). Mechanics are undecided (decision 0009); nothing here is canon.

- `bash game/g-d/ci.sh` runs every check and writes `bakeoff/g-d/reports/`.
- Play: `godot --path game/g-d` (keys in [mechanic.md](../../docs/bakeoff/mechanic.md#controls)).
- Replay with a trace: `godot --headless --fixed-fps 60 --path game/g-d res://tests/replay_runner.tscn -- --replay=res://replays/level01.replay.json --trace`.
- `bakeoff/{actions,resonance,greybox,replay,resonance_tag,tag_validator}.gd` and `tests/*.gd` are lane G-A's, copied with `jj file show -r bakeoff/g-a game/g-a/<path>` (the tests with `Game2D`/`Node2D` renamed to `Game3D`/`Node3D`). A G-A fix is re-copied the same way.
- The world is 1 m per tile on the z = 0 plane (docs/bakeoff/greybox-format.md); `GreyboxArt3D` draws toon-shaded cubes (the default art the replay and tag checks build with).
- Look: `art/lane_art.tres` is `KitArt3D` (`art/kit_art_3d.gd`), which dresses the level with the desert kit in `kit/` (copied from PR #31, see `kit/README.md`): sand tiles where a solid cell is open above and rock tiles beneath, solid 1 m tag-blocks tinted per mechanic.md on tagged cells (gray until the color is acquired; `art/tag_block.gdshader` adds stone grain, a gradient and a carved glyph, a diamond for red and a ring for green; lit only by a neutral white key, so they render close to the brief's colours; the warm sun, the fills and the blocks' own lights skip them), spikes, an orb pedestal that stays when the orb is taken, the goal gate, and seeded (20260927) mesas, arches, dunes, boulders, ruins, saguaros and acacias behind the play plane, hazed toward the horizon per layer, with a field of rolling dunes in the middle distance and a pale horizon layer of differently stretched mesas and arches further back, sand drifts at the foot of each step, small rocks, saguaros and scrub along the platform tops, a broad warm-to-cool colour drift across the ground, hazed acacia and saguaro silhouettes in the foreground, flapping birds, tiles varied in world-space bands of tone and edged with a dark lip where they are walkable, and the painted sky `art/sky.png` (`gen image`, glow-up round 1) on a quad that follows the camera, where the fogged sand meets it. Violet is drawn 0.6 m in front of the play plane with a smooth dark outline and a warm rim on her sun side, her body tinted darker than the sand, lit by her own warm key light and not by the tag-blocks' coloured lights, and her scarf a step lighter than the walls, so walls never hide or tint her. Ledge props sit on soft contact shadows. It changes no collider. `assets/bakeoff/desert-kit-3d/build_kit.py` and its sidecar are there only because the GLB sidecars name it as their input and `provenance check` follows inputs.
- Character: the painted Violet in violet-sprites v1 ([character-rig.md](../../docs/bakeoff/character-rig.md), "Painted sprite sequences"). `protagonist/sprites/violet.sprites.json` and its 86 frames (`protagonist/glow-up/trial-b/frames/`, the JSON's relative paths kept) are copied with their sidecars from `assets/bakeoff/protagonist/` (PR #36, and `phase1/violet-sprites` 9d17a872 for the JSON); the frames' provenance inputs are copied to `assets/bakeoff/protagonist/` so `provenance check` can follow them. `bakeoff/sprites.gd` reads the file and refuses a malformed one (push_error, no character; the game then quits with exit 1); `bakeoff/sprite_animator.gd` picks the animation from the player's state; `art/sprite_character_3d.gd` draws a body Sprite3D and a scarf Sprite3D over it, each with its anchor pixel on the player's ground point, 1.6 m tall, the scarf multiplied by the active color. Facing left mirrors each layer about the root rather than scaling the root by -1, which would turn the shaded sprites' normals away from the sun. The frames import with mipmaps, because they are drawn about 1/12 of their size.
- Sprite reader tests: `godot --headless --path game/g-d res://tests/sprite_test.tscn` (in `ci.sh`): frame index at boundary times, each layer's anchor landing on the root (measured from the sprite's drawn bounds), the scarf over the body, and malformed files refused, with a child Godot (`tests/sprite_attach.tscn`) showing the game exits non-zero.
- `ci-tools` lists the mise tools the bakeoff workflow installs for this lane.
