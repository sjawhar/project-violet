# Violet / Scarlet Design Archive, 2017-2019

This is the public design archive behind Violet's current reboot: the design documents, concept art, and prototypes produced across two earlier conceptions of the same game, before development paused in 2019. Sources are exports of Sami's personal Google Drive; a full research digest of every source file lives at [`docs/research/2026-09/archive-digest.md`](../research/2026-09/archive-digest.md) and its supplement at [`docs/research/2026-09/archive-supplement.md`](../research/2026-09/archive-supplement.md).

## Chronology

- **2017-12 to 2018-12 — "Resonate" (space version).** A CalArts/Coursera game-design specialization (`Learning/Game Design: Art and Concepts`) produced four capstone drafts and a slide-deck GDD for a space-pirate story: Violet raids an Academy ship, is captured, and is sent to recover a stolen Artifact that has bonded to her and grants her color-based powers.
- **2018 (undated, alongside the above) — early character/world sketches.** A separate `violet/` Drive folder holds standalone character and world documents exploring three different framings of Violet (swashbuckling pirate, Academy explorer paired with the armored Slate, unsure Academy novice) plus the real, shipped 64x64 pixel sprite animations.
- **2019-02-09 — the pivot.** A hand-drawn "idea tree" (`Ideation.pdf`) branches from a Platformer trunk into a Meditation -> Awareness -> Calm line — the seed of the mindfulness theme that becomes Scarlet.
- **2019 (MSU capstone course) — "Scarlet" (post-apocalyptic wasteland version).** A full design-document progression (high concept, competitive analysis, pitch, production schedule, story bible, GDD) reworks the premise: Scarlet is a devout Academy novice who is accidentally exiled into a drained, color-starved world and must find her way home, aided by an Academy agent named Slate.
- **2019-02 to 2019-05 — the Unity prototype (this repo's ancestor).** The `project-violet` Unity project (internally named "Buffalo" until its final commit) implements the resonance mechanic directly: three of four color abilities working, one level, no narrative content in-scene. Not part of this document archive; see the live repo history.
- **2026-09 — this archive.** The 2017-2019 material is organized here as reference for the current reboot.

## Two conceptions, compared

*(Copied from `docs/research/2026-09/archive-supplement.md`.)*

| | Space ("Resonate", 2017-18) | Post-apocalyptic wasteland ("Scarlet", 2019) |
|---|---|---|
| Protagonist | Hardened space pirate with a sister | Devout, content Academy novice |
| Engine of plot | Collar/leash quest to recover the Artifact | Accidental exile; search for the way home |
| World | Multiple planets, each with an elemental source | One drained world: desert Travelers living off Academy power stations, mines, forest, sunken temple, ruined Library city |
| Cost of power | Each world is left in chaos; memories of others are drained | World's energy drained by the Artifact; strange illnesses near it |
| Antagonist | High Scholar (explicit villain, boss fight) | None explicit; the Academy's doctrine itself |
| Slate | Academy agent/minder, becomes protective | Academy retriever in a metal suit, becomes an ally |
| Reveal | She merged with the Artifact; she's been draining memories | Library records the Artifact's origin and the Oneness philosophy |
| Ending | Sacrifice restores the worlds; Slate's sister | She dissolves into Being; the Artifact reappears; "Scarlet?" |
| Theme | Oneness as the walls of identity break down | Oneness/mindfulness; the stories we tell ourselves; grayscale fills with color |

**Thread that survives both:** the Academy (a colorless religion-science institution), the Artifact, Slate, the elemental/color abilities, merging with the Artifact at the end, and oneness as identity dissolving.
