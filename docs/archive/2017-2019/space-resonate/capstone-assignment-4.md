# Logline

Violet is trying to recover the powerful Artifact from her old pirate crew in exchange for her freedom from the Academy. In her journey across the stars, she displays mysterious new abilities with potentially disastrous effects.

# Synopsis

Violet is a hardened member of a crew of space pirates. During a raid on an Academy ship that was supposed to be routine, she finds the legendary Artifact, a powerful tool and weapon. It knocks her unconscious, and Violet's crew abandons her to be captured by the Academy. They fit her with a tracking collar, and the only way she can get it removed is to recover the Artifact that her crew stole. Violet's first move, of course, is to visit a black market fence on a volcanically active planet and get the collar removed. An Academy agent named Slate bursts in and chases her deep underground, where they encounter a source of elemental power. It reacts to Violet, causing destruction on the planet surface, giving her visions, and knocking her unconscious.

Slate takes Violet back to his ship. Now that the collar's off he has no choice but to stay close to her to ensure she recovers the artifact. Having only traded one collar for another, Violet resigns to continue the search. They travel to multiple planets chasing down leads, and at each planet they encounter an elemental source that reacts to Violet. Violet gains the ability to manipulate colors and perform amazing physical feats. She uses the power of colors and the elements to explore the worlds, but they leave each world in chaos. Violet is disturbed by the consequences of her actions. Slate suggests it must be due to her interaction with the Artifact; once it's been recovered, surely the Academy will set things right again.

Violet and Slate come to rely on each other, and they grow close as they learn more about each other. Slate's initial hostility towards Violet turns into protectiveness through their adventure. At first he claims this is because he is protecting the Academy's interests, though this increasingly seems just an excuse as their friendship strengthens. For her part, Violet's selfish pirate instincts soften as she visits the different worlds and connects with the people and their experiences, both positive and negative. However, Slate remains suspicious of the sometimes contradictory stories Violet tells about her past. On a space station, after interacting with the void of space itself, Violet becomes manic while telling Slate a story from her childhood. Slate is horrified: he has the exact same memory. To stop Violet from growing out of control, he knocks her out and takes her back to the Academy.

The High Scholar of the Academy experiments on Violet to try and understand her abilities. He reveals that she merged with the Artifact in her initial interaction; the crew didn't steal it after all. Seeing her tortured, Slate feels guilty and breaks her out. They escape the Academy together and make their way to Violet's home planet, as she sees visions of her sister in danger. When she gets to her, however, Violet's sister doesn't recognize her.

Violet is confused and distraught. Slate suggests they visit a planet with the ancient remains of the Academy. There, they learn how the Academy forged the Artifact to siphon and control the elements of the universe. Violet has been draining both those energies and the memories of those around her, explaining her inconsistent recollections of her past. The High Scholar attacks then, desperate to regain control of the Artifact. Slate and Violet defeat him. Violet then reveals that she must sacrifice herself to restore balance to the universe and undo the destruction they've caused. She does so, and the worlds are restored. Slate remembers his past and returns to his home planet to visit his sister, the woman who didn't recognize Violet.

# Genre and Market

This game is a 2D platformer with a strong focus on story and puzzle-solving. It explores the theme of oneness as the walls of Violet's identity break down. It should have a market in the indie platformer space.

# Gameplay

## Mechanics

This is a 2D puzzle platformer. Violet's interaction with the Artifact allows her to manipulate frequencies of light. She does so by "resonating" at a specific frequency, which the player sees as a shift in color. Each resonating frequency gives Violet different abilities. For example, she might be able to double-jump while blue, dash quickly while red, and stomp down with force while green. There are also special color platforms/walls that behave differently depending on Violet's resonance. For example, some platforms might only be solid if Violet is in the color corresponding to that platform, and there might be walls that turn invisible and can be walked through by shifting into their color.

Violet uses these abilities to traverse environmental puzzles. These puzzles sometimes require quick reflexes to rapidly shift between colors. For example, Violet might need to shift to blue to double jump over a high wall, then shift to red to dash mid-air through a red wall/opening, and finally shift to yellow before landing to stomp through a weak wooden floor. The levels are mostly linear, with little to no exploration. The focus is on environmental puzzle-solving and quick platforming.

# Inspiration Images

The Academy is an institution of both religion and science. Magic and technology merge and coalesce. It's also a place of strict rules and tradition, of control. It's a colorless world, literally.

> Image not included (third-party): a painted fantasy scene of magic-and-technology airships ("pixiv fantasia"). Source: http://jonvilma.com/images/pixiv-fantasia-12.jpg

The first image captures the combination of science and magic, and it has an appropriately muted palette. The painted style is also very much like what I'm going for. The ships look pretty close to what I would want the Academy's magically-powered spaceships to look like.

> Image not included (third-party): a mirror of the same pixiv fantasia scene. Source: https://i.warosu.org/data/tg/img/0377/47/1422723869906.jpg

The third image makes me think of what life is like outside the Academy. A shining city surrounded by desert. They hold themselves apart, out of arrogance, and others suffer in their shadow. The colorless, pristine city in contrast with the barren shore captures the dichotomy I'm going for. The city itself looks like a spaceport, though it needs a stronger archaic magic vibe.

> Image not included (third-party): a coastal high-tech city environment, "QR Coast Environment" by Daniel Graffenberger ("talros"). Source: https://cdna.artstation.com/p/assets/images/images/002/579/804/large/daniel-graffenberger-qr-coast-environment-by-talros-d6othyp.jpg?1463336641

Next up is an illustration of Violet by our super-talented artist (not me). You can see the mix of new tech and old style.

I imagine the head scarf being a strong visual element in the platforming gameplay. It will flow behind Violet as she jumps and dashes through the levels, and it will change color to indicate the current color/ability that Violet is using.

I'm not sure she'll actually have the spear, since combat might make the game a bit more complicated than I'm intending.

> Image not included (third-party): a barren ice-planet landscape ("Ice-Planet.png"). No source URL given in the source document.

Finally we have a world abandoned by the Academy. Whereas the desert in the previous image might still sustain life, here nature is inhospitable. It is a part of the universe long since abandoned. I imagine the player having to explore this area to learn the truth behind the Academy's beginnings.
