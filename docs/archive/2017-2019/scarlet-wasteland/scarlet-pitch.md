There is only Being. All being is one, and all beings are one. Sounds pretty good, right? Scarlet lives in the Academy, and the truth of Being is the only one she's ever known. A life of simple, quiet meditation. Oneness. Everything is… gray.

Until an accidental brush with a powerful Artifact in the secret heart of the Academy transports Scarlet to a colorful new world. Now on the run from the one and only church, school, and government in the known land, Scarlet finds herself with a powerful connection to the visual spectrum. Pursued by the Academy's enforcers, Scarlet jumps, dashes, slams, swings, and slides across strange and vibrant lands in this puzzle-based 2D platforming search for truth and the self.
