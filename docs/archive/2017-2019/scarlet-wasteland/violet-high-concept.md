# High Concept Statement

Violet is a fugitive from the Academy, the one and only church, school, and government in the known land. After a life in the Academy literally devoid of any color, Violet has an accidental encounter with a powerful Artifact that grants her a mysterious connection to the visual spectrum. Seeing color for the first time, she also finds herself with powerful new abilities. Violet jumps, dashes, slams, swings, and slides across strange and vibrant lands in this puzzle-based 2D platformer. Pursued by the Academy's enforcers, Violet searches for freedom and the truth.

Each level in Violet unveils a new color in the spectrum and a new ability along with it. The world grows ever more beautiful even as the environmental puzzles Violet overcomes become more and more challenging. Her growing power is not without cost, however. As she makes her way through the world, experience with those living outside the Academy's sway forces Violet to confront hard truths about life, the Academy, and herself. Her journey is one of revelation. The illusions are stripped from Violet's mind, layer by layer, even as the world continues to fill in with color.

The message of Violet is oneness and love. Just as colors are different frequencies of the same spectrum, so are all conscious beings manifestations of the same state of conscious awareness. By journeying with Violet as she lets go of the stories she's been told by the Academy, by those she meets, and by herself, the player is an active participant in her awakening.

# Feature Set

* Quick and tight platforming action
* Strong, engaging story
* Increasingly challenging environment puzzles
* Vibrant and light-based art style

# Team Roles

The team is still being formed, but we can identify key roles:

* As the entire team is new to video-game making, we will be placing special emphasis on story, art, and simple but engaging gameplay. As such, a lead game designer will be crucial for story-writing, keeping the team focused on the core principles, and ensuring a coherent final result.
* A digital artist will also be a key role, as the visuals and gameplay of Violet are tightly linked.
* Finally, a developer will be needed if the people filling the above two roles aren't able to handle the development on their own.

# The Competition

This game is competing not so much with other platformers as with other minimalist, story-driven indie games. The goal of Violet is to guide the player through a particular type of personal experience. We are not focusing on twitch gameplay, deep combat, punishing difficulty, or open worlds. As such, we draw inspiration from games like To the Moon, Never Alone, Life is Strange, Journey, Firewatch, and Ori and the Blind Forest.

# Innovation/Creativity

Violet's visuals *are* the gameplay in some ways, which we think is very unique. Violet's powers are based on different frequencies of light, and the way the world looks changes when she uses them. Puzzles will use this to hide or reveal important parts of the environment as Violet cycles through her powers.

Also, the story will attempt to make mainstream the concept of oneness and egolessness. The mental state we're trying to create in the player is not unlike one that might be achieved through meditation.

# Scope Management

We are limited by our experience, so we are not going to try to keep the gameplay and technical aspects of the game as simple as possible. We will not be developing mechanics more complex than acrobatics-based platforming. Combat is probably off the table, and possibly enemies or damage as well. Open world design is probably beyond our abilities at the moment, so the levels will be very linear. Inventory is unlikely. We're really going to focus on solid story and fun, simple, but challenging environmental puzzles.
