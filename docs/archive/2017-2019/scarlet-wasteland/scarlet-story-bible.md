# Introduction

Scarlet is a fugitive from the Academy, the one and only center of religion, science, and government in the known land. After a life in the Academy literally devoid of any color, Scarlet has an accidental encounter with a powerful Artifact that grants her a mysterious connection to the visual spectrum. Seeing color for the first time, she also finds herself with powerful new abilities. Scarlet jumps, dashes, slams, swings, and slides across strange and vibrant lands in this puzzle-based 2D platformer. Pursued by the Academy's enforcers, Scarlet searches for freedom and the truth.

# Settings

## The Academy

There is only Being. All being is one, and all beings are one. This is the Truth and law of the Academy. One might call it an institution of science, or of religion, or of law. The truth, however, is that it is *the* institution; there simply are no others. And within it, everyone and everything is the same. Its halls are populated by a uniform and androgynous people who have never seen the world outside its seamless steel walls. This is where we first meet Scarlet.

The main occupation and/or pastime of the Academics is quiet contemplation of the Truth. There is no other knowledge worth seeking. Those with the deepest understanding of the Truth guide and supervise life in the Academy. This is not a position of bestowed authority of course, as all beings are one, but simply a natural consequence of their Knowledge.

Unknown to all but the oldest and wisest Academics, a powerful Artifact lies at the center of the Academy. The Academics tend to it through rituals whose meanings have long since been lost. It is not known if these ministrations are responsible for the endless energy put forth by the Artifact - making life in the Academy possible - or if they are simply acts of thanks to a mysterious universe.

## Desert

Outside the Academy's walls, yet not so far as to be called remote, the nomadic desert people forge a life for themselves from the land. Where the Academics sit in quiet repose, the Travelers work and live and relish the living. They are not ignored by the Academics so much as simply forgotten, and there has been no interaction between the two people in memory or record. The Travelers have constructed power stations surrounding the Academy, feeding off the excess energies produced by the Artifact. They don't get too close, though, as stories of strange illnesses befalling those who do keep the rest at bay. A small merchant town has grown near the Academy. Groups of Travelers periodically return to this town to recharge and trade.

## The Fallen Library

Before the Academy, science and discovery flourished at the Library. While it started as a single, simple library, a small city eventually blossomed around this mountain-top center of learning. It was at the library where the Artifact was developed. Key to its development was the understanding of underlying universality of reality. Matter and energy, space and time, all simply different manifestations or interpretations of the same thing. Different ground states of a pervasive and all-encompassing field of being.

After the development of the Artifact, life became paradise. With infinite energy, all material needs were met. Further scientific progress became pointless. Over time, society folded in around the Artifact, and the Academy was born. The Library, still filled with ancient truths, was abandoned and soon forgotten.

Scarlet journeys to the Library to understand what's happened to her. By this point, the city hasn't felt a human heartbeat in hundreds of years. The elements have weathered the structures to complete disrepair. The skeletal frames of buildings dot the mountain side. The Library itself has fared somewhat better, but it is by no means an exemplar of structural stability. A monumental building, traversed in ancient times with the use of elevators and trains, it stands as Scarlet's biggest challenge.

## Other Locations

Each level in Scarlet unveils a new color in the spectrum and a new ability for Scarlet. The world grows ever more beautiful even as the environmental puzzles Scarlet overcomes become more and more challenging. Each level also represents one of the elements. The first, introduced above, is the desert (fire). Others include:

* Mines (earth)
* Forest (wind)
* Sunken temple (water)

# Plot

On her way to start the day's first meditation, a young woman stumbles upon a previously unnoticed doorway in the familiar halls. Entering, she finds herself in a room empty save for a beatiful, glowing sphere floating in its center. Entranced, she approaches it when voices behind her signal the approach of the leaders of the Academy. She backs into the Artifact, and a brilliant glow overcomes her. When she is once again in possession of her senses, the voices are louder and more concerned. Whatever happened, it's been noticed. Feeling panic for perhaps the first time in her life, the woman notices a patch of wall with a property she's also never seen before: color, a vivid red. Overwhelmed, she rushes towards it and away from the approaching Academics.

Seeming to walk right through the walls of the Academy, she emerges near a desert power station. A maintenance crew had been dispatched to this particular station after a sudden energy burst knocked it offline. Completely unprepared for the harsh sun and heat, Scarlet stumbles into the outpost before collapsing.

She is taken back to the main camp and cared for until she comes around. When she does, she finds herself in a vibrant new world. Completely engrossed, she decides to stay with the desert people. She helps her caretaker - Kuluun, an aging farmer - and other townspeople with their work, often volunteering to trek to the various outposts for needed supplies. In doing so, she finds herself able to dash short distances with astonishing speed, completing the trips in record time. In recognition of her help, the townspeople give the woman a scarf to protect her bald head from the sun (all Academics are bald from birth). This scarf has the same strange property as the mysterious patch of wall in the Academy. Inquiring, she learns about colors, though at the moment she only sees red. Everything else is gray. She takes the color of the scarf as her name: Scarlet.

It's not long before Scarlet becomes curious about the world beyond the desert, a concept that so recently would have been impossible for her to conjure. One day, strange metal men from the Academy invade the camp looking for Scarlet. Urged by Kuluun to escape into the mines, she dashes away into the unknown. The metal men pursue her through the mines. When a particularly treacherous ledge gives way behind her, one of the men falls with it. Scarlet risks her own life, using her dashing ability to save him. The other pursuers retreat.

The man, Slate, explains that the Academy has fallen into disarray. He demands that Scarlet come back with him to return the Artifact. Bewildered, Scarlet explains that she doesn't have it and never has. As the two argue and Scarlet grows increasingly frustrated, she starts to notice the walls of the mine twinkling in that strange, particular way as the color yellow enters her consciousness. In a moment of frustration, Scarlet stomps her foot with terrifying force. On ominous rumbling begins, and the two put the argument aside to escape from the mine.

They emerge on the edges of a forest. Scarlet convinces the man, Slate, that their only choice is to continue on together and try to find a way back. They trudge through the woodlands for a while with no clear progress when Scarlet has the idea to climb a tall tree for a better view. Distrustful of the world outside the Academy, Slate refuses to remove his metal enclosure and so is too heavy for some of the lighter branches. Scarlet reaches a vantage point, however, and notices a structure similar to the Academy near a body of water. At this exhilarating height, the beauty around her fills her mind and she learns of the color green.

As the two reach the structure, they discover it indeed resembles the Academy, though much smaller and now mostly submerged in a lake. The two are inevitably curious due to the similarity. However, Slate is unable to swim in his suit. Scarlet promises him that she will return after exploring the temple. She makes her way through its sunken halls with the help of her abilities, looking for another Artifact chamber. Instead, she finds writings about the development of the Artifact by scholars in a place called the "Library," atop a uniquely-shaped mountain. As she returns to the lake, she sees the blueness of its waters.

Scarlet explains what she found to Slate, and that she recognizes the mountain's description from the view she got above the trees. Perhaps there they might solve the mystery of the Artifact's disappearance? The two head in that direction, hiking higher and higher into the mountains. As they reach their target, they find the ruins of a small city. Traversing the carcass of this once-great center of learning is not without its dangers, but the two persevere and find themselves at the imposing doors of the Library. Inside, they learn of the origins of the Artifact and the philosophy of Oneness which came to dominate life in the Academy. In the highest levels of the Library, where the records indicate that the ancient scientists had their central insight, Scarlet pulls a lever. Unseen gearworks groan and complain as the observatory ceiling struggles open. A tremor shakes the skeletal remains of the library as spectral starlight shines down upon the wanderers. As Slate urges them to leave, Scarlet looks up peacefully and says: "There is only Being. All being is one, and all beings are one." She disappears. Slate only has a moment to react before the Library starts collapsing. He shakes off his surprise and dashes back the way he came, falling to his knees outside the structure only moments its total failure. He looks up to see Scarlet, perfectly calm. "I'll see you back home," she says, and disappears.

Much later, Slate arrives back at the Academy. He's ushered in with a hero's welcome. The Artifact is back! Bewildered, he enters the chamber of the Artifact and finds it floating in its customary place. His entourage return to their day's meditation, and Slate is left alone with the Artifact. He approaches it slowly.

"Scarlet?"

# Characters

## Scarlet

Scarlet lives in quiet reflection in a society where distinctions like "young" and "woman" are rarely mentioned. She looks like most Academics: dark-skinned, bald, and with barely a visual indication of gender. At the start of our story, she seems perfectly content with her life. After the incident with the Artifact, however, she begins to experience powerful emotions as she learns more about the world and herself. She's distraught by the thought that her actions are causing so much harm to those back at the Academy, and this drive to help is what pushes her forward.

## Slate

Slate is one of a handful of Academics tasked with bringing Scarlet back and solving the mystery of the Artifact's disappearance. He has no desire to be outside the Academy and sees danger in everything foreign. He's rigid and unyielding. Working with Scarlet is almost unbearable at first, though the two develop a respect and trust for one another through their journey and their shared goal of helping the Academy.

## Non-Player Characters

### Kuluun

Kuluun is a Traveler farmer. He's made a life for himself in the merchant town near the Academy by selling his cacti and trading with other Travelers who pass through. As the owner of one of the few permanent Traveler establishments, he takes Scarlet in after she's collapses near the outpost. Though gruff and finding Scarlet almost completely alien, he shows her kindness. He's good-natured at heart.

# Other Important Narrative Elements

## Game Mechanics

As Scarlet gains each ability, she also gains the ability to see that color. The game starts in black and white, and the color of the world "fills in" as the player makes her way through the game. This reflects the narrative in that the world becomes more colorful literally as Scarlet learns that life is much more "colorful" than the one of meditation she knew at the Academy. The journey teaches Scarlet fear, joy, anger, excitement, and much more.

The main movement mechanics are jumping, dashing (red), stomping (yellow), double-jumping (green), and swinging (blue). After using an ability, Scarlet sees the world in the frequency of that ability for a short time. This allows her to pass through barriers of that color. For example, dashing red turns the world red, which makes a red wall appear transparent and allows Scarlet to pass through it.
