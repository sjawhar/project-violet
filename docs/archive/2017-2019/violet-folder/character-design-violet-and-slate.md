Violet and Slate are the protagonists of a 2D platformer. In this world, there is an institution called the Academy which controls both religion and science in society. The style is therefore a mix of old and new.

Slate is the Academy's star, their prodigy. He's sent on a mission to recover an artifact of great importance. He's decked out with the academy's latest tech: armor, holo-shield, beam-sword, visor helmet thing. He's big and slow, but who needs to move fast when you're the chosen one?

Violet is a red shirt, nobody of significance. In contrast to Slate's heavy metal, Violet is all light, flowing cloth. Where Slate is enclosed in armor, Violet is open to the world. Where Slate fights, Violet explores. I imagine her eyes or hair being a bright violet, which is important to the gameplay.

Because I can't draw, I googled images of 2D platformer characters on which to base mine. Violet was based on a fan drawing of Lightning from FF13, and Slate on a 2D platformer asset pack.
