RESONATE - 2D PLATFORMER – FIRST LEVEL

Red lights flash, glowing hotly in the metal surface of the walls, the floor. VIOLET walks down the halls of a spaceship. This is a pirate vessel, all maverick patchwork and grit. A calm voice over an intercom:

THE CAPTAIN
We're almost in position. All breachers to your designated chute.

The player now takes control of Violet, learning how to move her back and forward. The ship jerks violently, and a couple crates tumble across the hallway floor.

THE PILOT
Umm, evasive maneuvers…

The player now learns how to jump. Violet humps over the crates and continues down the hallway. A door opens at her approach and she enters the breaching chute. A circular hatch is in the middle of the ceiling. Various pirates sound off on the intercom, announcing that they are in position. The hatch on the ceiling shines an X on floor. Violet moves to the center of the X.

VIOLET
Violet, in position.

The camera tracks up to reveal that on the other side of the hatch on the ceiling is outer space. Across an expanse of debris is another ship. In her chute, Violet touches a hand to her chest. A holographic helmet appears around her head, and small rocket boosters erupt from her boots. She floats off the ground slightly now.

THE CAPTAIN
Launching in 3, 2, 1…

The hatch opens, and Violet is sucked into the vacuum. The player dodges the space debris as Violet hurtles towards the other ship. As she nears it, Violet spins 180 and the camera turns with her. Her rocket boots fire and she slows, thudding onto the side of the ship. Violet's boots emit a flash of light.

VIOLET
Mag tether engaged.

Violet runs across the surface of the ship. Defense guns fire lasers at her, which the player must jump to avoid. The player learns how to dash and double-jump using the rocket boots. Violet makes her way to a hatch in the side of the ship. It opens and closes regularly. The player times their jump and enters the ship.

VIOLET
I'm in.

THE CAPTAIN
Good job, Violet. Now grab some loot and get out of there.

Violet explores the ship, which has branching hallways and many rooms. Some of the items the player can find include food in a store room, weapons in an armory, and shiny platinum currency. Violet has a backpack which fills up as she collects items.

THE CAPTAIN
Time's up. Let's clear out before backup gets here.

As the player heads back to where Violet entered the ship, there's an open door we haven't seen before. Something is glowing inside. Violet, intrigued, enters.

THE ARTIFACT floats in the center of the room. It's a small globe, about the size of a bowling ball, and it emits a clean, white light. Violet approaches it slowly. Another pirate, SCRATCH, enters the room.

SCRATCH
Didn't you hear the captain? It's time to go. Woah, that looks… expensive.

VIOLET
What is it?

SCRATCH
How should I know? Just grab it and let's go! Reinforcements are here.

As Violet reaches for it, a blinding flash of light. Violet is knocked away and collapses to the ground. Scratch looks around as alarms go off.

VIOLET
Help…

SCRATCH
Shit!

Scratch runs. Soldiers in gleaming gray enter the room, weapons drawn. Violet blacks out.
