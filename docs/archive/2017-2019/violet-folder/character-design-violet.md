Violet is a space pirate. That's way worse than a normal pirate, because SPACE! She's rough, she's ruthless, she has swords. Space swords!

The first inspiration is a bit on the nose: a violet. I had the idea of Violet having a partially shaved head with a tattoo on the shaved side. She's hiding knives behind her back. It looks like her hands ARE swords, but that's just because I can't draw.

Then I thought a bit more about those knives. Where would she keep them? That's when I found out about butterfly swords, which are easily concealed in the sleeve. So I drew a version of Violet wearing the kind of clothes that would let her slip knives up her sleeves. She also has a holographic helmet. Have I mentioned that she's in space?

The last image is inspired by a violin. Her leather jacket has the curves of a violin and the kind of buckles that would be on a violin case. I also tried to give her weapons on her hips that recalled the f-hole in a violin. And she has pins in her hair like tuning pegs. She also has rocket boots.

SPACE!
