The Academy is an institution of both religion and science. Magic and technology merge and coalesce. It's also a place of strict rules and tradition, of control. It's a colorless world, literally.

> Image not included (third-party): a painted fantasy scene of magic-and-technology airships ("pixiv fantasia"). Source: https://s3.amazonaws.com/coursera-uploads/peer-review/ca3359b75867731b769ebbf3d6c6bb81/pixiv-fantasia-12_1_.jpg

The first image captures the combination of science and magic, and it has an appropriately muted palette. The painted style is also very much like what I'm going for.

> Image not included (third-party): a photograph of St. Peter's Square, Vatican City. Source: https://s3.amazonaws.com/coursera-uploads/peer-review/954946df2d463e5f5dbd8378b73ac419/St_Peter_s_Square__Vatican_City_-_April_2007_1_.jpg

The second image, of Vatican City, is the closest thing I could think of to a real-life counterpart of the Academy. A whole city built around the Holy Roman Church. I think I could learn something from the way the city is laid out.

> Image not included (third-party): a coastal high-tech city environment, "QR Coast Environment" by Daniel Graffenberger ("talros"). Source: https://s3.amazonaws.com/coursera-uploads/peer-review/d1134f618b458f65d27e21e6c9ff87a9/qr_coast_environment_by_talros-d6othyp_1_.jpg

The third image makes me think of what life is like outside the Academy. A shining city surrounded by desert. They hold themselves apart, out of arrogance, and others suffer in their shadow. The depiction of the city in this image is a bit too high-tech, but the colorless, pristine city in contrast with the barren shore captures the dichotomy I'm going for.

> Image not included (third-party): a barren ice-planet landscape ("Ice-Planet.png"). Source: https://s3.amazonaws.com/coursera-uploads/peer-review/10b0b5755b124dcfa4e8c1ba6cd0e92e/Ice-Planet.png

The forth image would be somewhere abandoned by the Academy. Whereas the desert in the previous image might still sustain life, here nature is inhospitable. It is a part of the world long since abandoned. I imagine the player having to explore this area to learn the truth behind the Academy's beginnings.
