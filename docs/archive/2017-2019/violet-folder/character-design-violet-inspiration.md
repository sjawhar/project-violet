Violet is a young student at the Academy. In this world, the Academy serves as both the governing power and the center of religion and learning. As a test, Violet has been tasked to accompany and assist a party of Scholars on their quest to recover a stolen artifact. She is eager not only to make a positive impression on the Academy leadership, but also to learn more about the wider world. She is unsure of herself and her abilities and is searching for her calling in life.

DISCLAIMER: All designs were pulled from the internet and used as inspiration. Links to sources are included in the PDF. I did not draw these, because I no haz skill.

The first approach blends technology and ritual. Since the Academy is an institution of both religion and learning, it might be reflected in the garb of the students/scholars. The flowing robe looks old and ceremonial. I don't think Violet would have a weapon, though. I imagine her having some garment with a strong violet color (color is very important to this story), and the red in the robe is very striking. Perhaps Violet has a violet robe.

The second approach is much less dense and a bit more cartoony. The mood is less serious. Perhaps Violet has big, purple eyes like this drawing's big green eyes. Perhaps she wears a purple headband or necklace, which would stand out much more with this design.

The third approach is cute pixel art. This would work well for a retro 2D platformer type game. The teal headband and skirt really stand out in this design. I wouldn't give violet such a short skirt, though.
