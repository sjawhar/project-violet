# Art direction: 2.5D if agents can do it, otherwise painted 2D; flat vector acceptable

- **Date:** 2026-09-26
- **Status:** settled

## Decision

The preferred direction is D: a 3D-rendered world with 2D gameplay. If agents can't deliver it at quality, the direction is A, painted or hand-drawn 2D. C, flat graphic or vector, is also acceptable.

## Source

"It's D if agents can do it, otherwise A" and "C is also reasonable"

## Consequences

The direction is decided by evidence from the Phase 1 bake-off, not by assumption. Pixel art (option B) was not chosen.
