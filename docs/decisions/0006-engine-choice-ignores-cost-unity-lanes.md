# Engine choice ignores license cost; the bake-off includes Unity

- **Date:** 2026-09-26
- **Status:** settled

## Decision

Engine license and subscription costs do not factor into the engine choice. The bake-off includes Unity 6.3 lanes alongside Godot 4.7.2. Agents work in Unity only through Unity's official MCP.

## Source

"if you're going with godot because of cost reasons or something, please don't. I have money" and Sami chose "Yes, add Unity lanes"

## Consequences

Unreal was evaluated and ruled out for this project: disk and GPU requirements, binary assets, and Windows-only live coding. Third-party Unity MCP servers are excluded by Unity's Terms of Service, Section 17.2 (2026-06-30).
