# AI agents produce most of the work; Sami directs and approves

- **Date:** 2026-09-26
- **Status:** settled

## Decision

Agents produce most of the art, audio, writing, levels, and code. Sami gives feedback throughout and final approval on everything that ships. Player-facing AI-generated content is disclosed on Steam as Pre-Generated AI Content.

## Source

"It's going to be collaborative the whole way, I'll give lots of feedback and final approval, but yeah AIs will do most of it"

## Consequences

Every shipped asset needs a provenance record (tools/provenance) so the disclosure is accurate. Some awards exclude any generative AI, so Violet will be ineligible for those.
