# Story direction: name, look, companion, backstory, combat, and world

- **Date:** 2026-10-02
- **Status:** settled

## Decision

Round 1 of the story brainstorm settles six questions:

- **Her name:** back to Scarlet, using the 2019 naming logic, she names herself after her red scarf.
- **Her look:** keep the hair.
- **Companion:** Slate, a pursuer who becomes an ally.
- **Backstory delivery:** build it as "Memories of Others," a few fragments per chapter.
- **Combat:** none, as a deliberate choice.
- **World:** continue with the desert (wasteland) story as outlined, not the space version for now, though it needs a lot of work; the space idea is parked for later, not ruled out.

The ending, and the rest of the plot, are not decided. They stay open in the brainstorm with Sami and are not addressed by this record.

The desert story from the 2017 to 2019 "Scarlet" documents is the world the brainstorm now builds on for now; that story still needs substantial work before it's usable. The space "Resonate" conception is parked, not rejected, per Sami's own words.
