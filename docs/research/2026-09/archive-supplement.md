## New Violet material (9 files)
- **Resonate GDD.pptx** (2018-12-20) and **Capstone Assignments 1–4** (2017-12-30; four iterations of the same doc). These hold the complete **space version**:
  - **Logline:** Violet, a hardened space pirate, must recover the Artifact from her old crew in exchange for her freedom from the Academy.
  - **Setup:**
    - A routine raid on an Academy ship goes wrong. The Artifact knocks her out and her crew abandons her.
    - The Academy fits her with a tracking collar. She has a black-market fence remove it on a volcanic planet.
    - Academy agent **Slate** gives chase. They hit an underground elemental source that reacts to Violet, wrecks the surface, and gives her visions.
    - Slate becomes her minder: "traded one collar for another."
  - **Planet structure:** each planet has an elemental source. It grants a color power but leaves that world in chaos. Slate claims recovering the Artifact will set things right.
  - **Relationship:** Slate's hostility turns into protectiveness, and Violet's pirate selfishness softens. Slate grows suspicious of her contradictory stories about her past. On a space station, after touching "the void of space itself," she tells Slate a story from *his* childhood.
  - **Twist:** the **High Scholar** experiments on her and reveals she *merged* with the Artifact; the crew never stole it. Slate breaks her out.
  - **Sister:** she has visions of her sister in danger, but on their home planet the sister doesn't recognize her.
  - **Revelation:** on the ancient Academy remains they learn the Academy forged the Artifact to siphon the elements of the universe. Violet has been draining energy *and the memories of those around her*, which explains her inconsistent past.
  - **Ending:** they defeat the High Scholar. Violet sacrifices herself to restore the worlds. Slate recovers his memories and visits his sister, the woman who didn't recognize Violet.
  - **Mechanics:**
    - Early color mapping: **blue = double-jump, red = dash, green = stomp.** This differs from the later red/yellow/green/blue canon.
    - Some platforms are solid only in their own color; walls of a color turn invisible and walkable.
    - The **head scarf flows behind her and changes color to show the active ability**, a gameplay UI element.
    - Spear/combat was considered, then doubted.
  - **Art intent:** "painted style," muted palette for the Academy, "mix of new tech and old style." The Academy is religion + science, magic + technology, and colorless. There's a shining colorless city beside a barren desert shore, and an abandoned ice world where the truth of the Academy's origins lies. Vatican City is the real-world model.
- **Level Map.xml / Level Map Horizontal.xml** (draw.io): source files for the space version's level flowchart.

## Two conceptions, compared
| | Space ("Resonate", 2017–18) | Post-apocalyptic wasteland ("Scarlet", 2019) |
|---|---|---|
| Protagonist | Hardened space pirate with a sister | Devout, content Academy novice |
| Engine of plot | Collar/leash quest to recover the Artifact | Accidental exile; search for the way home |
| World | Multiple planets, each with an elemental source | One drained world: desert Travelers living off Academy power stations, mines, forest, sunken temple, ruined Library city |
| Cost of power | Each world is left in chaos; memories of others are drained | World's energy drained by the Artifact; strange illnesses near it |
| Antagonist | High Scholar (explicit villain, boss fight) | None explicit; the Academy's doctrine itself |
| Slate | Academy agent/minder, becomes protective | Academy retriever in a metal suit, becomes an ally |
| Reveal | She merged with the Artifact; she's been draining memories | Library records the Artifact's origin and the Oneness philosophy |
| Ending | Sacrifice restores the worlds; Slate's sister | She dissolves into Being; the Artifact reappears; "Scarlet?" |
| Theme | Oneness as the walls of identity break down | Oneness/mindfulness; the stories we tell ourselves; grayscale fills with color |

**Thread that survives both:** the Academy (a colorless religion–science institution), the Artifact, Slate, the elemental/color abilities, merging with the Artifact at the end, and oneness as identity dissolving. The "Memories of Others" idea the Scarlet Story Bible called unbuilt is **fully built in the space version**: draining memories is the central mystery and the reason her sister doesn't recognize her.
