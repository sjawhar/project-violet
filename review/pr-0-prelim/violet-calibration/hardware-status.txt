Violet bake-off lanes: Radeon 890M frame times

Measured 2026-10-01 on machine sami (AMD Radeon 890M, Mesa 26.1.6; display 3440x1440 at 165 Hz) in real fullscreen desktop windows (XWayland), vsync off.
Each run plays the lane's level01 replay end to end (about 67 s) with the lane's final art (--art=res://art/lane_art.tres); the first 30 frames are dropped.
"1080p" renders at 1920x1080 (Godot content scale, stretch mode viewport) and scales to the screen; "native" renders at 3440x1440.
Budgets: 16.7 ms per frame at 60 Hz, 6.1 ms at 165 Hz.

Before the painted-Violet port (heads g-a e25ea497, g-d dc6224e6, g-c 2f5dede9), two 1080p trials and one native run:
  g-a: 1080p median 1.91 / 1.97 ms, slowest 1% 7.22 / 7.28 ms, 464 / 458 fps; native median 3.61 ms, slowest 1% 11.79 ms, 251 fps (gl_compatibility, AMD Radeon 890M Graphics)
  g-d: 1080p median 2.39 / 2.43 ms, slowest 1% 8.58 / 8.73 ms, 381 / 374 fps; native median 4.74 ms, slowest 1% 15.01 ms, 195 fps (forward_plus, AMD Radeon 890M Graphics)
  g-c: 1080p median 1.3 / 1.3 ms, slowest 1% 5.82 / 5.52 ms, 660 / 670 fps; native median 2.69 ms, slowest 1% 9.66 ms, 331 fps (gl_compatibility, AMD Radeon 890M Graphics)

After the painted-Violet port (heads g-a 2b431500, g-d 514e445d, g-c 7b96988f):
  g-a: 1080p median 1.89 / 1.91 ms, slowest 1% 7.12 / 7.03 ms, 474 / 470 fps; native median 3.66 ms, slowest 1% 11.5 ms, 248 fps
  g-d: 1080p median 2.24 / 2.27 ms, slowest 1% 8.41 / 8.08 ms, 404 / 400 fps; native median 4.54 ms, slowest 1% 15.08 ms, 203 fps
  g-c: 1080p median 1.26 / 1.28 ms, slowest 1% 5.22 / 5.47 ms, 686 / 673 fps; native median 2.67 ms, slowest 1% 9.45 ms, 334 fps

Reading: every lane is far inside 60 Hz even in its slowest 1% of frames. At 165 Hz the typical frame fits easily, but the slowest 1% (5 to 9 ms at 1080p) would occasionally miss a refresh in G-A and G-D. The painted Violet did not change the cost measurably.
Caveat: the after-port runs shared the machine with other agents' headless Godot jobs; they match the quiet before-port runs within about 0.15 ms median.

Earlier private-Xvfb probes (no desktop window) could not reach the Radeon: Vulkan had no presentation support (no DRI3) and OpenGL fell back to CPU llvmpipe.
