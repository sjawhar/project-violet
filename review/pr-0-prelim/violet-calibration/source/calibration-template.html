What changed, and what still shows

Improved

  • Painted action poses are less rigid than the original cutout rig.
  • Darker body values separate the robe from the gray background.
  • Idle is steadier, with anchored feet and a small breath and weight shift.
  • The scarf has more fabric texture, and the landing scarf stays on one trailing side.

Still limited

  • Cloth, hair and character details change between drawings instead of moving consistently.
  • Scarf tips and some feet clip at the capture edges.
  • The sprite animations have no runtime reader yet. This page is rendered media, not a playable character.
  • Hardware timing is unmeasured. The existing request is on the studio task.

This is calibration art, not approval to replace the production character or the master design. Round 10 is the end of this study, not a claim about the best result the project could ever reach.

How to read the scores

Scores are visual quality / character appeal / color readability. Different critic sessions scored the same art differently, sometimes by a point or more. Read each within-round comparison and the visible result, not a rising leaderboard. These numbers are not human approval.

All historical critics through round 9 and the technique trial actually ran Anthropic Claude, despite their “astra” labels. They were not an independent model-family check on the earlier workers. @@FINAL_MODEL@@
