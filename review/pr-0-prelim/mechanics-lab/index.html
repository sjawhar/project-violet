Violet mechanics lab

Throwaway greybox. Each experiment asks one question for the mechanics brainstorm; nothing here is canon.

Play in the browser Linux build (zip)

Answer the questions in issue #41. A gamepad works in both builds; the browser needs a click on the game before sound starts.

Experiments

  1. Movement feel
    Does this feel right as the baseline? Tab compares with the bake-off's movement; F1 tunes it live.
  2. Ability triggers resonance
    The 2019 design: using an ability makes its color resonate for 1.5s. Does a dash into a red wall read as 'passing through', not a glitch?
  3. Choose your color
    The bake-off design: switch cycles a persistent active color, and only its ability works. Is losing the other ability while red is selected annoying or fine?
  4. Hold to resonate
    A third option: abilities always work, but resonating costs a held breath meter. Does metering the 'pass through' moment feel better or worse than the other two?
  5. Yellow stomp
    Is stomping through weak floors fun enough to be a color's whole ability?
  6. Blue as teleport
    Blue was undecided in 2019 between swinging and teleporting. Does a short teleport feel different enough from dash?
  7. Blue as swing
    Does swinging add a kind of movement the other colors don't have?
  8. Your 2019 example puzzle (ability triggers resonance)
    "shift to green to double jump over a high wall, then shift to red to dash mid-air through a red wall/opening, and finally shift to yellow before landing to stomp through a weak wooden floor." Does it work as a moment?
  9. Your 2019 example puzzle (choose your color)
    "shift to green to double jump over a high wall, then shift to red to dash mid-air through a red wall/opening, and finally shift to yellow before landing to stomp through a weak wooden floor." Does it work as a moment?
